# CIC convention comparison figure

`mu1_training_convention_scan_vs_nbody.pdf` overlays the held-out μ1 mean and realization scatter with three training-only power-model conventions: CIC on both clustering and shot noise, CIC on clustering with a plain shot term, and no CIC. The `CIC both` curve is the convention selected for diagnostic comparison because the observable is a CIC-assigned count field and its Poisson contribution is filtered by the same mesh window.

The curves use a fiducial training fit at `k<=0.08 h/Mpc`; the held-out mean is never used to fit b1 or shot noise. The PDF uses a sign-preserving symlog y-axis so the large-separation zero crossing remains visible.
