# μ1/μ₂ training best-fit overlay figure

The one-page PDF `mu1_mu2_training_bestfit_vs_measured_symlog.pdf` overlays all three node curves on common axes. The upper panel is μ1; the lower panel is the Gaussian μ₂ component. Colored lines are projections of each node's training-only mean `P_h(k)` (`real000`--`real069`), and matching colored points with standard-error bars are the held-out means (`real070`--`real099`).

A sign-preserving symlog y-axis is used because both observables cross zero at large separation. The μ1 held-out χ²/dof values are 15.57, 16.83, and 19.00; μ₂,G values are 7.41, 7.03, and 7.69 for fiducial, LC_m, and LC_p. These are leakage-free diagnostics, not production analytic mean curves: μ₂ contact, clustered halo, and primordial four-point terms are not included.
