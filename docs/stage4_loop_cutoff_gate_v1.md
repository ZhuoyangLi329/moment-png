# Stage 4 loop cutoff gate

The same Ngrid=64, bias inputs and quadrature (n_q=n_\mu=24) were used for the qmax and qmin scans. The mean held-out RMS pull over fiducial, LC− and LC+ for qmax (=0.03,0.05,0.08) is 23.81, 196.46 and 1578.74 for the (s=40\)--80 shells, respectively.

The fundamental qmin scan reduces the change relative to (q_{\min}=10^{-4}), but it remains material and the qfund total held-out \(\chi^2/\mathrm{dof}\) is 162.3, 374.6 and 69.2 for fiducial, LC− and LC+. Thus qmax (=0.03) is retained as the least discrepant diagnostic anchor, with no cutoff promoted to production.
