# Disjoint PNG response shape basis diagnostic

The frozen one-template response fit gives `chi2/dof=207.04`.  Flexible empirical stress tests reduce this to `46.97` with a log-tilt and `46.18` with an inverse-squared scale basis; a three-parameter polynomial residual remains `219.67`.  These bases are diagnostic only and are not halo bias operators.  The residual therefore cannot be closed by a simple amplitude re-normalization, and the independent `b_phi` source remains blocked pending a validated scale-dependent response theory and b1 cross-covariance.
